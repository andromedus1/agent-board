moshi core
nothing extra to install
A local-first terminal that already does the work.
Moshi connects directly to your own Mac, Linux machine, WSL box, or VPS over SSH, Mosh, or ET. There’s no session relay: your shell, repositories, files, and agent processes stay on machines you control.
You don’t need a wrapper command or host daemon. Open the same shell you use at your desk, then use the controls a terminal needs when it lives on a phone.

Now make the terminal agent-aware.
Install moshi-hook on your machine to add structured agent context to the same live terminal session. It doesn’t replace Claude Code, Codex, or your shell.
Terminal, diff, file, and preview traffic still travels directly between your phone and your machine. The hook adds the native surfaces that make it easier to step away and stay in the loop.

Why Moshi instead of Claude Code Remote Control?
Remote Control continues a Claude conversation. Moshi gives you the whole machine: every agent — Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and more — with a real shell underneath. Run commands, tail logs, drive tmux or Zellij, browse the repo, review diffs, and preview dev servers, all in the same live session you sit back down to at your desk.
See all comparisons 
Why Moshi instead of an agent IDE like Orca?
Agent IDEs run your agents inside their own daemon, reach your phone through their own relay, and only work with their own mobile app. Moshi keeps your agents in tmux or Herdr and connects straight to your machine over SSH, Mosh, or ET, so you can attach from any terminal and switch tools any time. Then it adds what a native app should: Live Activities, Apple Watch approvals, terminal gestures, and on-device dictation, all from a 13 MB desktop app.
