## Prior-art and substrate posture

The existing Codex topology and Claude feasibility briefs are framing lenses only.
They are never citation targets. Herdr is a newly surfaced acquisition target and
will be engaged through its current official documentation and source repository.

## Confirmed decomposition rationale

The operator confirmed three evidence lanes on 2026-08-20:

1. the current Codex lifecycle and control surface;
2. the current Claude Code lifecycle and control surface; and
3. Herdr's status-authority, detection, persistence, and control architecture.

This provider/source-owned decomposition was selected over a lifecycle-layer split
or topology-alternative split because it keeps acquisition and attestation
ownership clean and permits independent runtime probes. Its known risk is that a
provider-shaped decomposition can smuggle provider capabilities into the common
contract. The parent cross-synthesis must therefore evaluate every lane against
Agent Board's existing provider-neutral state, evidence, capability, and
acknowledgement primitives rather than treating signal identity as the definition
of symmetry.
