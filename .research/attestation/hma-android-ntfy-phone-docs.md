## Summary

ntfy documentation for the Android and iOS apps.

## Key passages

- [1] "You can get the Android app from Google Play, F-Droid, or via the APKs from GitHub Releases... the F-Droid flavor does not use Firebase."
- [2] Instant delivery works "even when your phone is in doze mode" using a foreground service shown as a permanent notification; "Without instant delivery, messages may arrive with a significant delay (sometimes many minutes, or even hours later)".
- [3] "The ntfy Android app uses Firebase only for the main host ntfy.sh, and only in the Google Play flavor of the app. It won't use Firebase for any self-hosted servers."
- [4] Priority levels map to Android notification channels with per-priority sound and vibration; high and urgent notifications show as pop-over.
