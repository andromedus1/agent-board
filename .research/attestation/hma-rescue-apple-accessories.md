## Summary

Apple Support, 'Allow USB and other accessories to connect to your Mac' (Published April 30, 2026).

## Key passages

- [1] "When you use a new or unknown USB accessory, Thunderbolt accessory, or SD card with your Mac laptop with Apple silicon, you get an alert that asks you to allow the accessory to connect."
- [2] Settings under Privacy & Security > Accessories: "Always ask", "Ask for new accessories" (default), "Automatically allow when unlocked", "Always allow".
- [3] "If your Mac is locked, you need to unlock it before you can allow an accessory to connect." Locked 3+ days may require re-approval.
