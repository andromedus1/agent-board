## Summary

Moshi team article (dated August 6, 2026) comparing Android terminals for coding agents. It is vendor-authored and says so. It states what Android does not get, requirements, and its claims about Termux, Termius, JuiceSSH and ConnectBot.

## Key passages

- [1] Disclosure: "This piece is written by the Moshi team — we disclose that up front and still try to be honest about where we lose."
- [2] "Some surfaces in Moshi's docs are iOS-specific: Live Activities, Dynamic Island, and the Apple Watch companion. On Android you still get the terminal, Agents feed, push, and host-side hooks."
- [3] Install requirement cited: Android 10+. Pricing quoted in the article: "$7.99/mo, $69.99/yr, or a one-time $199 lifetime license."
- [4] Free vs Pro per the article: "Mosh, multiplexer pairing, image paste, diff viewer, and browser preview are Pro."
- [5] JuiceSSH: "its last release shipped in February 2021, and the app was unpublished from Google Play in December 2025."
- [6] Comparison table cell for ConnectBot reads Mosh: "No (SSH-oriented)"; for Termius reads Mosh: "Yes (client support)"; Termux Mosh: "Possible via packages, not the product focus"; Termux has no agent inbox or hooks.
- [7] "Mosh (and similar resilient transports) is what keeps an interactive session usable on a phone" because Android suspends apps aggressively and hops Wi-Fi to cellular.

## Structural notes

- Vendor-authored comparison; competitor cells are the vendor's, not independently tested. The ConnectBot Mosh cell conflicts with the ConnectBot Play listing (see hma-android-connectbot-play).
