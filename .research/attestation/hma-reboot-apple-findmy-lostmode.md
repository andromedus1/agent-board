## Summary

Apple Find My User Guide page "Turn on Lost Mode for a device in Find My on Mac".

## Key passages

- [1] "To mark a device as lost, you must add it to Find My before it’s lost."
- [2] "The device is locked with a passcode so no one else can open it."
- [3] "Important: You can’t unlock a Mac online or on another device. You can only unlock it on the Mac you marked as lost."
- [4] Status note: "Will be enabled when online: If the device isn’t connected to a Wi-Fi or cellular network when you mark it as lost, this message appears until the device goes online again."
