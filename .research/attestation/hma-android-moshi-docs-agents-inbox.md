## Summary

Moshi documentation of the Inbox/Agents board and Usage screens populated by moshi-hook: status sections, approval actions, decay rules, filters, and grouping.

## Key passages

- [1] "With several agents in flight, the Inbox reads like a kanban board: Needs you at the top, Working under it, Done below that." "On iPhone the Inbox sits one swipe to the left of Home. On iPad and foldables it sits beside Home."
- [2] "Approvals and questions — agent stopped to ask permission or for an answer. Approve or deny a waiting prompt right from its row in the list; tap the row to read the full prompt or pick from a longer list of options on the detail screen."
- [3] "Each row names the machine it came from, with the icon you chose for that connection, plus the project and model." New events for the same session collapse into one row.
- [4] Decay: a pending approval stays active until answered or the host times it out; a completed turn stays active for 10 minutes; anything older than 6 hours auto-archives.
- [5] Filter by Agent, Host or Project; on iPhone, Group by switches rows from Status to Host or Project.
- [6] The Inbox "stay[s] synchronized between your iPhone and paired Apple Watch" (the page's platform framing is Apple).

## Structural notes

- Vendor documentation; the page frames the board around iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch and does not state an Android layout.
