## Summary

Moshi documentation page Free vs Pro: what ships free, what Pro adds, and billing across App Store, Google Play and web.

## Key passages

- [1] Free tier includes unlimited active sessions, up to 2 saved connections, SSH, agent-event push notifications (up to 10 fan-outs per 60 seconds), biometric key protection, usage tracking, and "Inbox — first 5 actions free as a trial (approve, deny, answer), then Pro."
- [2] Pro adds mosh ("The single biggest reason to upgrade"), ET, multiplexer integration for tmux, Herdr and Zellij, auto-attach after reconnect for tmux and Herdr, image paste, diff viewer, browser preview, "Unlimited inbox actions", a higher push rate (60 per 60 seconds) and unified push.
- [3] "Apple Watch — view-only mirror of inbox and usage. iOS only."
- [4] Billing: store purchases are billed by Apple or Google; web purchases by Stripe; cancel Google subscriptions from the Google Play app.
