## Summary

Apple Support article "How to log in automatically to a Mac user account". Lists conditions under which the automatic-login setting is dimmed and manual login is required.

## Key passages

- [1] "If the automatic login setting is dimmed or doesn't show the account name, then manual login is required. For example, manual login is required in these circumstances: FileVault is turned on."
- [2] Same list: the Mac is supervised with a profile that prohibits automatic login; or "The account is set up to use an Apple Account password to log in."
