## Summary

Moshi pricing page, current version: Free plus Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime Pro, with licence sharing across iOS and Android.

- [1] Page title "Moshi Pricing — Free Terminal, Pro from $9.99/mo"; Monthly $9.99 / month, Yearly $89.99 / year (about $7.50/mo), Lifetime $199 once ("limited time" offer).
- [2] "Every plan is full Pro on up to 3 devices." "Your Pro entitlement follows the account that bought it, and a Moshi license key shares it across up to 3 devices — including across iOS and Android."

- [4] "Store purchases are billed by Apple or Google... Web purchases are billed by Stripe." Regional pricing is set by the stores.
