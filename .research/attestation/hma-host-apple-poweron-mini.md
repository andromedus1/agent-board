## Summary

Apple Support article "Turn on a Mac mini, Mac Studio, or iMac without pressing its power button" (Published Date June 15, 2026). Describes the "Start up when power is connected" Energy setting.

## Key passages

- [1] System requirements: "macOS 26.5 or later and one of these computers: Mac mini introduced in 2024 or later; Mac Studio introduced in 2025 or later; iMac introduced in 2024 or later".
- [2] "From the Apple menu, choose System Settings. Click Energy in the sidebar. From the 'Start up when power is connected' menu on the right, choose Always."
- [3] "Your Mac will now automatically turn on whenever you connect it to power ... It will also automatically turn on when power is restored after a power failure."
- [4] "If your Mac has remained connected to power since it was shut down, it's best to wait about 30 seconds between disconnecting and reconnecting power".

## Structural notes

- The older "Start up automatically after a power failure" label is not described on this page.
