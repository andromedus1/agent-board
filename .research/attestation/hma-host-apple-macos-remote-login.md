## Summary

Apple Mac User Guide page "Allow a remote computer to access your Mac". Remote Login (SSH/SFTP) setup and user restriction.

## Key passages

- [1] "Turn on Remote Login to access your Mac from another computer using SSH (Secure Shell Protocol) or SFTP (SSH File Transfer Protocol)."
- [2] "Choose who can log in to your computer: Click the pop-up menu next to \"Allow access for,\" choose \"Only these users,\" click at the bottom of the list, select users who can log in remotely".
- [3] "If you want remote users to have full access to the disk on your Mac, turn on \"Allow full disk access for remote users.\""
- [4] "Allowing remote login to your Mac can make it less secure."

## Structural notes

- Page is the macOS 27-era Mac User Guide as served on 2026-11-10; wording may differ from the macOS 26 edition.
