## Summary

Moshi documentation of licensing and sharing: how store or web purchases become a Moshi licence and how devices, including Android phones, join it.

## Key passages

- [1] "Agent pushes can fan out to every opted-in device on the license, so your iPhone and Android phone can both receive the same approval or completion alert." "Each device still keeps its own local app settings, notification permission, and OS-level push token."
- [2] "Restoring on Android requires the Google account that bought Pro." Joining license-scoped sharing across iOS and Android uses the licence key and counts toward the 3-device limit.
- [3] A Google Play purchase is verified by sending the purchase token to the Moshi API, which verifies it with Google.
